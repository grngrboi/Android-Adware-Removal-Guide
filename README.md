# Android Adware Removal Guide

A straightforward, step-by-step tutorial to hunt down and remove stubborn adware or full-screen pop-up ads from your Android phone using Android Debug Bridge (ADB). 

As a software engineering student, I created this guide to provide a technical workaround for deeply hidden adware that standard uninstalls or DNS blocks cannot fix.

## Why Use This Method?
* **No Factory Reset:** Fix the issue without wiping your phone or losing photos, contacts, and notes.
* **Targeted Removal:** Exposes the exact hidden package name of the virus.
* **Official Tools:** Uses Google's official, free ADB tool.

## Requirements
* A Windows computer
* A data-capable USB cable (not just a charging cable)
* The affected Android phone
* About 15 to 20 minutes

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer & Risks
* **Do Not Guess:** Only uninstall packages you are absolutely certain are adware. Accidentally removing a core system app (like your phone's launcher or `com.android.systemui`) will crash the phone and force the exact factory reset you are trying to avoid.
* **Turn Off USB Debugging:** Once you are done, you must turn off USB Debugging in Developer Options. Leaving it on exposes your phone to security risks when plugged into public computers or charging stations.
* **Use AI Assistance:** If you get confused by the command line interface or developer options, use tools like ChatGPT or Gemini to help clarify the steps.

## How to Access the Guide
1. Click on `Android_Adware_Removal_Guide.pdf` in the files above.
2. Click the **Download raw file** button (the download icon on the right side) to save the PDF to your device.
3. Follow the instructions sequentially.
